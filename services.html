Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do you use templates?

No. Every site is designed from scratch to fit you, so it does not look like anyone else's. That is the whole point.

What about the domain and hosting?

Your first year of domain and hosting is included in the price, and we handle the technical setup. Hosting on our setup is fast and low cost, so there is no big monthly bill waiting for you.

How long does it take?

Most projects go live in days to a couple of weeks, depending on size and how quickly you get us your content and feedback.

How does payment work?

Half to start, half at launch, arranged directly with the studio by transfer or invoice. Payment is never taken through this website.

Do I own the finished site?

Yes. On final payment the site and its design are yours.

Can you keep my project private?

Absolutely. If you would rather your site not appear publicly in our work, just say so and we will keep it private or shown on request only.
