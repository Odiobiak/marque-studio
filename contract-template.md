## 6. Client responsibilities
The Client agrees to provide, in usable form and in a timely manner:

## 7. Ownership & handover
- On **final payment**, the Studio assigns to the Client all rights to the final delivered

- Until final payment is received, all work remains the property of the Studio.
- The Studio may retain and reuse general techniques, know-how, and non-client-specific
  components in other projects.

## 8. Third-party materials
