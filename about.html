About the studio

We are a small studio that cares about your website almost as much as you do.

M

Marque Studio is an independent design studio. We make beautiful, bespoke websites for people who are proud of what they do and want the internet to show it. Portfolios, personal brands, weddings, and events.

We started Marque for a simple reason. Most people are handed a choice between a cheap template that looks like everyone else's, or an agency quote that costs more than a small car. We wanted to sit in the middle. Real, custom design, done with care, at a price a real person can say yes to.

Every site is designed from a blank page to fit you, then built, launched on your own domain, and looked after. You do not need to learn anything or manage anything. You tell us about your world, and we hand you something you are proud to share.

Small studio, high standards, and a genuine person on the other end of the email. That is the whole idea.
